Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about my development process, services, or how I can help your business grow? Here are answers to the most common questions I receive from clients and potential partners.

1. What technologies do you specialize in?

I specialize in modern full-stack development with React.js, Next.js, Node.js, and Express.js. On the database side, I work with both MongoDB and MySQL. For mobile development, I use React Native. All my projects are built with clean, production-grade code focused on scalability and performance.

2. Do you use drag-and-drop builders or templates?

No. I don't use drag-and-drop builders or pre-made templates like WordPress. Every project I build is custom-coded from scratch. This ensures your solution is optimized for your specific needs, fully scalable, and built with proper, production-grade code that's easy to maintain and extend.

3. What types of projects do you work on?

I work on a wide range of projects including custom websites, web applications, mobile apps, software solutions, content management systems, and digital products. Whether you need a simple website or a complex full-stack application with multiple frontends and a single backend, I have the expertise to bring it to life.

4. How do you approach project timelines?

I believe in transparent communication and realistic timelines. Every project is unique, so I assess your requirements thoroughly and provide accurate estimates. I prioritize quality over speed—rushing leads to bugs and technical debt. You'll receive regular updates on progress, and I ensure on-time delivery without compromising code quality.

5. Do you provide support after project completion?

Absolutely! I provide ongoing support and maintenance for all my projects. This includes bug fixes, updates, performance optimization, and adding new features as your business grows. My goal is to ensure your solution remains secure, scalable, and aligned with your evolving business needs long after launch.
